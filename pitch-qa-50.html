Part 1 — Questions I answer

What a PSC judge asks the founder. Say the gold line, then stop.

Mapped to the PSC scorecard: Viability 25% · Relevance 25% · Impact 20% · Creativity 20% · Presentation 10%. 5-minute pitch, 5-minute Q&A — every answer below fits in one breath.
1I answer · Viability
What problem are you solving?›

Owners know their sales but not their profit. SeePat tells them what they really earned.

2I answer · Viability
Who exactly has this problem?›

Sari-sari, karinderya, palengke vendors — 1.1 million microenterprises, 9 of 10 PH businesses.

3I answer · Viability
How do you know the problem is real?›

DTI found only 6% of MSMEs use advanced digital tools — and our pilot interviews ask owners to state their profit.

4I answer · Viability
What do they use today instead?›

Notebooks, memory, GCash history — scattered, never connected into one picture.

5I answer · Relevance
What is your solution, simply?›

A phone app: speak or snap, and it tells you your profit, your utang, and what to do next.

6I answer · Relevance
What have you built so far?›

A working app I can demo today — 407 automated tests, not slides.

7I answer · Relevance
Who pays, and how much?›

App is free forever. PRO is ₱99/month — only for internet parts like cloud backup.

8I answer · Relevance
Who is your first customer?›

Coops and LGUs first — one signature reaches 10–20 stores they already trust.

9I answer · Creativity
Who are your competitors?›

Peddlr, Lista, Packworks — good at recording. None explains what the numbers mean.

10I answer · Creativity
What makes you different?›

We answer “nasaan ang puhunan ko?” — where your money sits — nobody else does that.

11I answer · Creativity
Why can't ChatGPT do this?›

ChatGPT can't see her books. We compute from her real records — offline, for free.

12I answer · Impact
What impact will this bring?›

Shops that know their profit survive — that is jobs kept and SDG 8, 9, 10 in action.

13I answer · Impact
How will you measure that impact?›

% of owners who can state their profit at exit, utang collected, stores still recording at week 8.

14I answer · Impact
How will you scale beyond the pilot?›

Through coops and LGUs — partners owners trust, not one-by-one selling.

15I answer · Viability
What is your pilot plan?›

30 Dingle stores, free for 8–12 weeks — we set up, visit weekly, measure everything.

16I answer · Viability
What would make the pilot a failure?›

Fewer than half still recording at week 8 — or slower than the notebook.

17I answer · Relevance
What traction do you have now?›

Honestly: working prototype, pilot next. No invented users — judges trust empty tables over fake ones.

18I answer · Viability
What is your biggest risk?›

Speed. Slower than the notebook means nobody uses it — so the pilot times every transaction.

19I answer · Team
Why is your team the one to build this?›

We live where our users live — SIPAT LABS, ISUFST Dingle. Visits are free, fixes ship same-day.

20I answer · Impact
Why should DICT care?›

Filipino ICT innovation that works with no signal — proven today, not promised.

21I answer · Impact
Why should DTI care?›

Every store I onboard is one MSME digitized — DTI's own “Tindahan Mo, e-Level Up Mo!” goal.

22I answer · Impact
Why should DOST care?›

SETUP-ready upgrade for the smallest firms — productivity with before-and-after numbers.

23I answer · Relevance
How much does the pilot cost?›

Within our filed ₱150,000 — no new money asked. Stores are never paid to join.

24I answer · Presentation
What changes for the owner tomorrow?›

She opens the app: today's profit, who owes, what to restock — and the one thing to do first.

25I answer · Ask
What are you asking for?›

A free 30-store pilot — plus intro letters to coops and LGU livelihood offices.

Part 2 — Questions about the system

What a technical judge asks to check I'm not bluffing. Same 2-line rule.

26System
How does it work, step by step?›

She records → app computes → it explains → she decides. Nothing saves without her tap.

27System
Can the AI invent wrong numbers?›

No. AI only explains — every peso comes from fixed math on her real records.

28System
How is profit computed?›

Each sale deducts its buying cost instantly — daily profit with no shelf-counting.

29System
What is the Business Twin?›

A live picture of the shop: cash, stock, who owes her, profit — updated every sale.

30System
How does voice recording work?›

“Sipat, nakabenta ako…” becomes a draft she confirms — prices always need her OK.

31System
Does voice work offline?›

Yes — a small 42MB model downloads once on Wi-Fi, then runs fully on the phone.

32System
How accurate is the Taglish voice?›

About 4 of 5 words right — so we match her item list, and she confirms every price.

33System
How does receipt scanning work?›

Picturan lang ang resibo — app drafts the purchase, she checks every line.

34System
How does it handle utang?›

Utang is money owed — never counted as cash. Who owes, how much, collect-first nudges.

35System
What is the SeePat Score?›

Shop health out of 100 — profit, cash, stock, utang, gastos, steadiness. Never a credit score.

36System
Is family money mixed with store money?›

No. Money taken home is “kinuha” — profit stays about the shop.

37System
What works with no internet?›

Everything daily — records, answers, voice, scan. Only cloud backup needs internet.

38System
Where does her data go?›

Nowhere. The ledger never leaves her phone — no account, no upload.

39System
How do GCash and Maya fit?›

Separate pockets — GCash money is not drawer money. Deep reconciliation: not yet built.

40System
What phones does it run on?›

Any Android with a browser — installs like an app, works offline once saved.

41System
How do you know the numbers are correct?›

The app re-checks its own balance after every transaction — 407 tests prove it.

42System
Why is giving it away free sustainable?›

Free parts run on her phone — ₱0 cost per use, so ₱0 price forever.

43System
Will ₱99 subscriptions pay a salary?›

No — and we say so. Coops and LGUs carry revenue; subscriptions prove willingness to pay.

44System
What if she deletes something by mistake?›

Nothing is ever deleted — mistakes are voided, history stays, anything can be replayed.

45System
What is deliberately NOT built?›

Servers, printers, hardware. No screen promises a room that was never built.

46System
Can you demo it live right now?›

Yes — hand me a real receipt and real Taglish, and we run it.

47System
Does it print receipts or handle taxes?›

No — it's a management tool, not a BIR POS. Deliberate, and stated upfront.

48System
What breaks if 1,000 stores join?›

Nothing on their phones — free tier has no server to overload.

49System
How will owners pay you?›

GCash — the money they already use. Pilot measures the real mix.

50System
Why now — why hasn't this existed?›

Cheap phones can finally run voice AI offline at ₱0 — that unlock didn't exist before.
