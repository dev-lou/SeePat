# Customer interview pack — SeePat

**Why this exists.** The PSC XI screening committee asked for two things in one sentence:

> *"Conduct customer interviews and present the findings."* — DICT screening committee, PSC XI
> evaluation and feedback form

This is the instrument: a consent script, a question guide aimed at the five information gaps the
concept note is built on, a recording template, and the data-protection rules that apply when a
student team interviews a sari-sari owner in her own shop.

**The findings go in [`INTERVIEW_FINDINGS.md`](INTERVIEW_FINDINGS.md)**, which is deliberately empty
until real interviews happen. A team that shows invented findings loses more credibility than one
that shows ten honest ones.

---

## 1. Before the interview

**Who to interview.** Target 12–15 owners for the first round, chosen for variety rather than
convenience:

| Segment | Minimum | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sari-sari store, *utang* notebook in use | 6 | The core user, and the one with the collecting problem |
| Carinderia or food stall | 3 | Daily margins, spoilage, and no inventory count at all |
| Market vendor (*palengke*) | 3 | Cash-heavy, no fixed premises, different recording rhythm |
| Owner already using digital tooling (Peddlr, Lista, a spreadsheet) | 2 | The comparison that keeps the pitch honest |

**Where.** The barangays around ISUFST Dingle Campus, plus a public market. One interview per owner,
20–30 minutes, at her counter — not at the campus, and not over the phone.

**What to bring.** This guide, two copies of the consent script, a notebook, and the prototype on a
phone (for the last five minutes only).

**The discipline that matters most.** Do not pitch. The moment an owner knows what the app does, her
answer to "how do you decide what to restock?" becomes a review of the app instead of a description
of her week. The prototype stays in the pocket until the guide is finished.

## 2. Consent script (read aloud, verbatim)

> **Filipino —** Magandang araw po! Kami po ay estudyante ng ISUFST Dingle Campus at gumagawa kami ng
> proyekto para makatulong sa maliliit na tindahan. Hihingi po kami ng 20–30 minuto ng oras ninyo
> para magtanong tungkol sa pang-araw-araw na pagpapatakbo ng tindahan — benta, gastos, paninda,
> utang. Wala po itong bayad, at hindi po kayo kailangang sumagot ng kahit anong ayaw ninyo.
>
> Hindi po namin isusulat ang pangalan ninyo o ng tindahan ninyo. Ang mga sagot ay ilalagay sa isang
> buod na walang pangalan. Puwede po kayong tumigil o magpasya na huwag ituloy kahit sa gitna ng
> usapan. **Pumapayag po ba kayo?**
>
> *(Kung sasabihin nilang maaari kaming mag-record ng boses para mas tumpak ang tala:)* Gusto ninyong
> i-record ko ang usapan para hindi ako magkamali sa pagsusulat? Puwede ring hindi — nagsusulat na
> lang ako.

> **English —** Good day! We are students of ISUFST Dingle Campus working on a project to help small
> stores. We would like 20–30 minutes of your time to ask about how you run the store day to day —
> sales, expenses, stock, *utang*. There is no payment, and you do not have to answer anything you
> would rather not.
>
> We will not write down your name or your store's name. The answers go into a summary with no names
> in it. You may stop, or change your mind, at any point. **May we continue?**
>
> *(Only if she offers, or agrees when asked:)* Would you like me to record the audio so I do not
> mis-write your answers? We can also skip that and I will just take notes.

**If she says no,** thank her, ask nothing further, and leave. Record the refusal in the log — a
refusal is data about the approach, not a failure.

## 3. The guide

Grouped by the five gaps the concept note names. Ask open questions, then follow the money: every
answer that mentions a number is worth a follow-up ("magkano ho iyon?", "how much was that?").

### A. How the store runs today (warm-up, 5 minutes)

1. Ilang taon na po ang tindahan? Anong oras po kayo nagbubukas at nagsasara?
2. Sino po ang tumutulong sa inyo — pamilya, empleyado?
3. May notebook po ba kayo para sa benta? Puwede ko po bang makita kung paano ninyo ito sinusulatan?

### B. The profit gap — *Magkano talaga ang kinita ko?* (5 minutes)

4. Kung tatanungin ko po kayo ngayon: magkano ang **kinita** ninyo kahapon — hindi ang benta, kundi
   ang natira pagkatapos ng gastos at puhunan? *(Let her answer. Note whether she can, and how long
   it takes.)*
5. Paano po ninyo nalalaman kung kumikita pa ang isang paninda? May alam po ba kayong presyo na
   talo na pala?

### C. The capital gap — *Nasaan ang puhunan ko?* (5 minutes)

6. Kung bukas po ay ititigil ninyo ang tindahan, magkano ang makukuha ninyo sa lahat — pera sa kahon,
   paninda, utang sa inyo, kagamitan, at ibinayad na sa supplier? *(Note which of these she names
   without prompting. This question is the product's whole positioning.)*
7. Ang perang kinukuha ninyo para sa bahay — gastos po ba iyon ng tindahan, o hiwalay?

### D. The cash-flow gap — *Bakit mataas ang benta pero kulang ang cash?* (5 minutes)

8. May linggo po ba na malakas ang benta pero kulang ang pera? Ano po ang nangyayari?
9. Nasaan po ang pera ninyo ngayon — sa kahon lang, o may GCash o Maya din? Paano po ninyo
   natatrack ang nasa e-wallet?

### E. The inventory and decision gaps — *Ano ang mauubos, at ano ang bibilhin bukas?* (5 minutes)

10. Paano po ninyo nalalaman na malapit nang maubos ang isang paninda? Nagbibilang po ba kayo?
11. Ano po ang binili ninyo sa huling delivery, at bakit iyon?
12. May paninda po ba na matagal nang nakatambak? Ano ang balak ninyo dito?

### F. Utang and collection (5 minutes — this is the ranked #1 gap)

13. Ilan po ang may utang sa inyo ngayon, at mga magkano lahat? Paano po ninyo nasusubaybayan?
14. Paano po kayo sumisingil? Nakakalimutan po ba? May nasisingil po ba kayong hindi na?

### G. Willingness to pay, and close (last 5 minutes)

15. Kung may app na titingnan ang lahat ng ito sa phone — benta, gastos, paninda, utang, at
    masasagot ang "nasaan ang puhunan ko?" — magkano po ang makatwirang bayaran bawat buwan?
    *(Options are not offered. Let her name a number first, then:)* Mas mura po ba kung isang bayaran
    na para sa isang taon?

**Then, and only then,** show the prototype on a phone for 3–5 minutes and ask one question: *"Ano
ang unang hindi ninyo maintindihan?"*

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | | | | | | | | | | | | | | | | |

**Fields that must not be recorded:** her name, the store's name, her address, her number, her
customers' names. The quote column exists so the pitch can show a real sentence without identifying
anyone.

## 5. Data protection (RA 10173), for a student team doing fieldwork

- **Collect the least you can.** The table above is the whole data set. Age, exact income and family
  details are not needed for this pilot and should not be written down.
- **The consent is the record.** Keep the dated sheet that says she agreed; if she refused, keep only
  the refusal count.
- **Audio is optional and deleted after transcription.** If she agreed to a recording, transcribe it
  the same day and delete the file. Do not keep audio of a shop conversation on a phone.
- **Storage.** Notes live in one shared file with no names, not scattered across five phones. If the
  team keeps the raw sheets, they stay with one named custodian.
- **Sharing.** Findings may be cited as counts and anonymised quotes. No owner's figures go into a
  pitch deck, a grant application or a paper without her separate, explicit permission.
- **Withdrawal.** If an owner asks to be removed after the interview, remove the row. Ask once, at the
  end of the interview, whether she wants a copy of what was written.

## 6. What the findings must answer

The pack produces evidence for exactly these questions, and the write-up should state each answer
with the count behind it:

1. Can an owner state her profit without counting? *(If most cannot, the product's core claim is
   validated.)*
2. Which components of capital does she name when asked where her money is? *(Drives the capital
   view's defaults.)*
3. How much *utang* is outstanding, and is collection a stated problem? *(The ranked #1 gap.)*
4. Does she separate household money from store money, or is that a distinction she has never had a
   tool for? *(Validates the withdrawal design.)*
5. What will she actually pay, and will she prepay a year? *(Replaces the model's assumption in
   [`REVENUE_PROJECTION.md`](REVENUE_PROJECTION.md) with a measured number.)*
